### 🧠 Brain & Nervous System

* **Neurology** – Diseases of the brain, spinal cord, nerves.
* **Neurosurgery** – Surgical treatment of brain, spinal cord, nerves.
* **Psychiatry** – Mental health, emotional and behavioral disorders.
* **Psychology / Clinical Psychology** – Non-medical therapy and behavioral health.


### ❤️ Heart & Blood

* **Cardiology** – Heart and blood vessels (medical).
* **Cardiothoracic Surgery** – Surgery on heart, lungs, chest.
* **Vascular Surgery** – Surgery on blood vessels (excluding heart & brain).
* **Hematology** – Blood diseases (anemia, leukemia, clotting disorders).


### 🫁 Lungs & Breathing

* **Pulmonology (Respiratory Medicine)** – Lungs, asthma, COPD, tuberculosis.
* **Thoracic Surgery** – Surgery on chest (excluding heart).


### 🧑‍⚕️ General & Surgical Fields

* **General Medicine (Internal Medicine)** – Adult medical care (non-surgical).
* **General Surgery** – Abdominal and other common surgeries.
* **Trauma & Emergency Medicine** – Emergency and accident care.
* **Critical Care / Intensive Care (ICU)** – Life support and critically ill patients.


### 🦴 Bones, Joints & Muscles

* **Orthopedics** – Bones, joints, ligaments, muscles.
* **Rheumatology** – Arthritis and autoimmune joint diseases.
* **Physical Medicine & Rehabilitation (Physiatry)** – Recovery, physical therapy.


### 🍽️ Digestive System

* **Gastroenterology** – Stomach, intestines, liver, pancreas.
* **Hepatology** – Specialized in liver diseases.
* **Colorectal Surgery / Proctology** – Diseases of rectum, anus, colon.


### 🧑‍⚕️ Kidneys & Urinary

* **Urology** – Urinary tract & male reproductive system.
* **Nephrology** – Kidney diseases (dialysis, chronic kidney disease).


### 🧬 Hormones & Metabolism

* **Endocrinology** – Hormonal disorders (diabetes, thyroid, etc.).
* **Metabolism / Nutrition** – Obesity, diet-related conditions.


### 👩 Women’s Health

* **Gynecology** – Female reproductive system.
* **Obstetrics** – Pregnancy and childbirth.
* **Reproductive Medicine / IVF** – Infertility treatments.


### 👶 Children’s Health

* **Pediatrics** – General child health.
* **Pediatric Surgery** – Surgeries for children.
* **Neonatology** – Care of newborns, especially premature babies.


### 👁️👂 Head & Senses

* **Ophthalmology** – Eye diseases & surgery.
* **Otolaryngology (ENT)** – Ear, nose, throat.
* **Audiology** – Hearing and balance.
* **Dentistry / Oral & Maxillofacial Surgery** – Teeth, jaws, mouth.


### 🧴 Skin & Appearance

* **Dermatology** – Skin, hair, nails.
* **Plastic & Reconstructive Surgery** – Cosmetic and repair surgery.


### 🧪 Other Specialties

* **Oncology** – Cancer treatment.
* **Radiology** – Imaging (X-ray, MRI, CT, ultrasound).
* **Nuclear Medicine** – Radioactive-based imaging/treatment.
* **Pathology** – Disease diagnosis via lab tests, biopsies.
* **Anesthesiology** – Pain relief, anesthesia for surgeries.
* **Family Medicine** – General health for all ages.
* **Occupational Medicine** – Work-related health.
* **Sports Medicine** – Sports injuries and fitness health.
* **Palliative Care** – End-of-life and pain management.
